Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock chapters?
Chapters can be unlocked using coins. The first 5 chapters of every book are free! You can earn coins by daily check-ins, reading streaks, and completing achievements. Enable auto-unlock in Accessibility settings for a seamless reading experience.
How do I customize my character?
Go to your Profile tab and tap "Edit Character Profile" to customize your character's appearance including name, hair color, eye color, skin tone, and more. Your character details will be reflected in the stories you read!
How do I earn more coins?
You can earn coins through:
  • Daily check-ins (50 coins)
  • Reading streaks (10-100 coins)
  • Reading time rewards (10 coins per 10 minutes)
  • Completing achievements
  • Watching reward videos (coming soon)
Can I read offline?
Yes! Once you've unlocked a chapter, it's saved to your device for offline reading. You can enjoy your books anywhere, anytime.
How do I report a bug or issue?
Please email us at support@romanceme.app with a description of the issue, your device type, and app version. Include screenshots if possible to help us resolve the issue quickly.
Is my data secure?
Yes! We take your privacy seriously. All personal data is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your information with third parties without your consent. Read our full Privacy Policy for more details.

Troubleshooting

App won't open or crashes

Try these steps:

  1. Force close the app and restart it
  2. Check for app updates in the App Store
  3. Restart your device
  4. If the problem persists, reinstall the app (your account data is saved)

Login issues

If you're having trouble logging in:

  • Check your internet connection
  • Verify your email address is correct
  • Use the "Forgot Password" option to reset your password
  • Contact support if you continue to have issues

Coins not updating

If your coin balance isn't updating:

  • Check your internet connection
  • Pull down to refresh on the Rewards screen
  • Log out and log back in
  • Contact support with your account email
