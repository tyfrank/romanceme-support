Data Retention

We retain account information while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to provide the service, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and comply with law. When you use Profile > Delete Account:

  • Your RomanceMe account, active sessions, and associated app records are deleted from the primary service.
  • Limited transaction, security, backup, or legal records may remain where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the service.
  • Information previously processed by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, AdMob, or Sentry is governed by those providers' retention practices.
  • Aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably identify you may be retained.

Advertising Tracking and Your Choices

On iOS, RomanceMe requests permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before allowing tracking for advertising. You may deny the request and still use the App. You can change the choice later in iOS Settings. On Android and iOS, device privacy and advertising settings may also limit ad personalization. Rewarded ads may still be available as contextual or non-personalized ads when tracking is not allowed.
